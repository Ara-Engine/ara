# Beauty verification content

Powered by [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com). The beauty scene arranges these
authored models and their original base-colour, normal and ORM textures:

- [Sofa 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/sofa_03)
- [Mid Century Lounge Chair](https://polyhaven.com/a/mid_century_lounge_chair)
- [Coffee Table Round 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/coffee_table_round_01)
- [Potted Plant 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_04)
- [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor)

All are [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The pinned manifest records each artist, source URL and SHA-256 for every input.
The authors retain the source attribution listed there; Ara's room arrangement
and lighting are original. This is a renderer fixture, not a complete asset importer.
Large model/texture files stay under ignored `.sn/assets/beauty`; run
`fetch-beauty.ps1` to obtain the exact content without Blender or Python.

The Sponza benchmark remains separately fetched with its original `copyright.txt`;
it is not represented as CC0. ReSTIR and the denoiser are Ara implementations
based on published algorithms; no NVIDIA SDK source or binaries are redistributed.
