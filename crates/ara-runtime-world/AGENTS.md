# ara-runtime-world

World streaming in play: the `.world` (ara-world) a scene names in its
`StreamedWorld` component streams its cells into the running simulation around the
players, at the session's streaming boundary. Depends on ara-world, ara-runtime,
ara-sim, ara-physics.

## Public API

- `WorldStreaming::from_workspace(workspace, scene)` → `Option<(plugin,
  Receiver<CellEvent>)>` (none when the scene streams no world). Install it **last**
  among runtime plugins: `register` clones the registry into the world workers (rows
  are prepared against it).
  - `before_tick`: interest from every `PlayerControl` entity's `WorldPose` (and
    `Velocity` for lookahead), in player order; `Streaming::update` at tick + 1.
  - `stream`: `Streaming::activate` inside `Session::step_with_streaming` (64 entities
    per boundary; arrivals are recorded in the tick record and replay exactly).
  - `mirror`: a networked client's never-stepped mirror streams around its own player
    through `Session::mirror_streaming` at the presented authoritative tick.
  - `restored` (after `Runtime::restore`/`replay`): a fresh streaming state, `Removed`
    events for the cells that were resident, and every cell with rows in the restored
    world kept wanted in adoption mode (`Streaming::adopt_resident`) until resident
    again: its rows count as spawned, nothing spawns twice.
  - `settled(timeout)`: waits for in-flight cooks before each boundary
    (`Streaming::settle`), so cells arrive at the first boundary after they become
    wanted whatever the worker speed: same seed + inputs → same hash across runs.
    Headless players settle; windowed play streams live.
  - A cell that fails to cook is reported once as a runtime error.
  - `is_resident(cell)`, `manifest()`.
- `CellEvent::{Ready, Removed}` (cell ID + `CellKey`), in activation order: consumers
  load and unload per-cell presentation data (baked `AcousticCell` probes via
  `Cell.acoustics`).
- `SnapshotSource`: `CellSource` cooking cells from the snapshot's in-memory documents
  (`cook_cell`, no HLOD) on the world worker threads. References resolve against the
  snapshot's asset index (`PlayScene.imports`).

## Runtime support for streamed entities

ara-runtime adopts entities spawned after build: `physics.adopt` poses new simulated
bodies, `animation.slots` gives animators their slot, `navigation.adopt` gives agents
and obstacles their state, `ai.adopt` starts brains, `terrain.chunks` cooks
`TerrainChunk` heightfield bodies, and `Runtime::new` decodes the models of every
snapshot document (decoding never happens in a tick). With a `Terrain`, navigation
follows the resident chunks (`navigation.stream`). Networked authorities stream
through `SessionAdapter::stream`; replication carries streamed entities to clients,
whose mirrors stream the same cells locally.

## Gotchas

- Cell scenes are separate documents; the played level must not also contain them.
  Streamed identities (record IDs) must be unique across the level and every cell.
- Keep the player's start chunk in the level (always resident), so it has ground and
  navigation at tick 0.
- A cell inside its unload grace at a checkpoint restarts its grace after a restore.

## Testing

`cargo test -p ara-runtime-world` (event forwarding) and `--test valley`: the
integration level (`ara-player/demo/levels/valley.scene`, its terrain, foliage, world,
cells, baked acoustics, behaviour, music, sounds and rock model) is generated by
typed commands and canonical writers (`ARA_UPDATE_DEMO=1` rewrites it), then played
in-process: cells stream in ahead of the Fox, it stays grounded across the border,
navigation swaps to the resident chunks, the guard hunts, and two runs, an exact
checkpoint and a recording all agree. Networked over MCP:
`cargo test -p ara-player --test valley -- --nocapture`.
