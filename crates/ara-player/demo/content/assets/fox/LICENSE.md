# Fox

`Fox.glb` is the Khronos glTF Sample Assets "Fox" model
(https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox).

- Low poly fox model by PixelMannen: CC0 1.0.
- Rigging and animation by @tomkranis on Sketchfab: CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- glTF conversion by @AsoboStudio and @scurest.

Used unmodified as the demo character (animations `Survey`, `Walk`, `Run`).
