| D32 | **Screen probe GI over the probe volume ("Lumen alternative", raster High/Epic).** One probe per 16x16 render-pixel tile, placed on the G-buffer at the tile centre, traces 64 fixed hemi-octahedral directions through a Hi-Z (closest-depth) pyramid of the frame's depth. Hits shade from last frame's colour pyramid at the ray cone's footprint, minus emission the host promoted to emitter lights (`look::EMITTER_RADIANCE`), capped at what a diffuse surface of the hit's albedo sends (last frame's colour holds view-dependent reflections). Misses take the probe volume's parallax-corrected radiance, then the sky. Each probe keeps its traced radiance, four GGX-prefiltered levels and its cosine irradiance. Pixels gather a 3x3 tent of probes on their plane at half resolution: diffuse from the irradiance, glossy lobes from the split sum, rough lobes (roughness above 0.5) by the true view-dependent GGX lobe over the probe's directions. This replaces probe, capture and bitmask-AO terms where the probes cover; SSR fades out under them past roughness 0.25–0.4. Multi-bounce, emissive surfaces on screen and near-field occlusion follow from the hits. Everything is fixed by the frame: no jitter, no history, no denoiser (D27's no-noise gate). SSR traverses the same Hi-Z pyramid. Reflection captures were tried as the miss fallback and rejected: they came out darker than the reference on Polar and black in a test room. The probe volume's indoor overshoot is fixed (2026-11-01: leaks through thin walls, relight points floating between surfaces, octahedral solid angles, unmapped cascades, misses recorded at 200 m, backfacing from the biased point): ten test scenes average 0.127 mean log error of region radiance against the path tracer with screen GI off, 0.381 before. The accuracy pass (2026-11-01: the sun over 2x2 footprint points on the texel's own surface, direct light only for its hits on the relit plane, captures without promoted emitters' glow) brings them to 0.098 (0.143 before). Screen GI still ships off by default on every preset, re-measured against that: enabled, the ten scenes average 0.127, worse than probes alone on Polar (0.21 to 0.26), the panel room (0.14 to 0.15), the sunlit room (0.10 to 0.16), the sky room (0.11 to 0.17), the sky-lit box (0.13 to 0.14) and open ground (0.06 to 0.09, 0.09 to 0.16), equal in the glass rooms and courtyard. Causes measured: rough lobes at grazing views renormalised over the traced hemisphere brighten open ground 4-10%; rough-metal ceilings read brighter (sky room 1.31x to 1.55x, box 1.11x to 1.37x); its 16 px probes mottle where few of their 64 rays see a window. It fails the acceptance (match or beat probes on every scene). Tuned settings stay `SCREEN_GI_HIGH`/`SCREEN_GI_EPIC`. Re-measured after the reflections pass (2026-11-01) against path-traced references that now compensate GGX's multiple scattering as Cycles and the raster do (the old ones lost up to half a rough metal's reflection): probes alone average 0.098, with screen GI 0.106. Screen GI is better in the panel room (0.123 to 0.095), the sunlit room (0.142 to 0.080) and the unglazed room (0.060 to 0.055), equal in the courtyard and glazed room, worse on Polar (0.192 to 0.240), the sky room (0.124 to 0.130), the sky-lit box (0.126 to 0.137) and open ground (0.060 to 0.091, 0.086 to 0.159). It still fails the acceptance and stays off. The open-ground excess is diffuse, not the rough lobe (reflections are about 1% of that ground's radiance): no 4x4 irradiance texel faces the probe normal, and the tilted ones spread the sky's mean over their below-horizon share. Evidence: [plan](plan/rendering-production.md), Global illumination. | 2026-11-01 |

## Architecture (adopted from research)

These are working decisions. A spike result or a new entry here can change them.

| ID | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Object model.** Entities in a hierarchy with plain reflected components in `bevy_ecs`. *Behaviours* are per-entity C# objects that hold no authoritative state; *Systems* are batched queries with declared reads/writes, scheduled in parallel. Prefab overrides are addressed by ID. | R01, R10 |
| A2 | **Schema DB is the single source of truth.** Rust derives and C# source generators feed it. It carries units, ranges, required docs and replication policy, and generates serialization, the inspector, bindings, JSON Schema, the MCP catalog, docs and net codecs. Implemented in M1 as an Ara-owned `#[derive(Schema)]` (crates `ara-schema` + `ara-schema-derive`), independent of bevy_ecs; `bevy_reflect` is optional for runtime inspection once ECS lands. | R01, R03, R07, M1 |
| A3 | **Files.** Canonical strict JSON; defaults omitted; a flat entity map keyed by short IDs; `@path#id` references; `ara fmt` and `ara merge`. Binary only for source media. Derived data lives in a content-addressed cache. | R01, R03 |
| A4 | **Mutation.** Typed command → validated ops with automatic inverses → transaction attributed to a human or an agent. Agent edits apply live but are shown as unreviewed until accepted or reverted. Network gameplay commands are intents, never edits. | R01, R03, R04, R09 |
| A5 | **Engine Protocol.** JSON-RPC 2.0 over WebSocket/stdio, localhost plus token; the editor uses it too. A generated MCP adapter exposes about 12 tools. | R01, R03 |
| A6 | **Processes.** One engine library; `ara editor`, `ara` (CLI/daemon) and `ara-player`. Play mode runs in a child process. Importers are pure functions in worker processes. | R01, R03, R04 |
| A7 | **UI stack.** Retained tree; Taffy layout; Parley text, with HarfBuzz via FFI wherever coverage is missing; engine display lists; AccessKit. HTML-like markup plus a CSS-subset stylesheet with typed C# bindings. Focus and input in the style of Unreal's CommonUI. | R05, R07, R10 |
| A8 | **Rendering.** SM6.6-style bindless; explicit render-graph barriers; multi-queue. Slang → SPIR-V, and Slang → DXIL → Metal Shader Converter → metallib. One material AST with a text view and a graph view. Tiers: RT, Raster, Mobile; baked/probe lighting is mandatory as the fallback. | R02, R08 |
| A9 | **Platform layer:** SDL3 via `sdl3-sys`. | R08 |

## Development Rust reload (S11)

**A14 (2026-10-27):** opt-in development Rust hotpatching uses Dioxus Subsecond
and Bevy ECS's experimental hotpatching dispatch. Publish at quiescent frame/tick
boundaries, preserve last-good code on rejection, and rebuild for data-layout or
unsupported retained-code changes. Patched simulation sessions are explicitly
tainted for replay/networking. Shipping/export players exclude this feature.
The Windows rust-lld facade and initial Windows/Mac evidence are in
[`S11`](../spikes/s11-rust-hotpatch/RESULT.md). This does not change D17's toolchain
or add dx as a build prerequisite. Windows render/ECS/editor paths preserve their
PID and state; S11 records their measured latency and current limitations.
